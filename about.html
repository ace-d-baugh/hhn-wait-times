About

Why this exists, and how to use it

I made this because I wanted to see the HHN house wait times in one easy glance instead of opening Universal's app, tap through, and wait to load for each house individually. Just one screen, all the houses, updated live.

How to use it

  • Wait times update automatically every 30 seconds. No need to refresh.
  • Sort the houses by Wait Time, Alphabetical, or Custom order from the dropdown up top. The A→Z / Z→A button next to it flips the direction.
  • Tap a house to mark it done once you've gone through it. It grays out and gets a checkmark. Tap it again to undo.
  • Long-press and drag a house to put it wherever you want. This switches Sort to Custom automatically so your order sticks.
  • A red ▲ or green ▼ next to a wait time means it went up or down since the last check.
  • Everything you set (sort order, custom positions, houses marked done) is remembered on your device, so it's still there next time you open the page.

Houses & Attractions

Houses Attractions

Use the pill switch in the top-right corner (the house / roller coaster icons) to flip the whole board between the HHN haunted houses and the regular attractions Universal keeps open during the event. Your sort order, custom arrangement, and completed checkmarks are remembered separately for each, so switching back and forth never mixes them up.

A house that hasn't opened yet for the night shows when it will (either an early-access time or the general event start, whichever applies to that house) right on its card.

Shows

Orlando's two nightly stage shows, Stranger Things: Return to Hawkins (lagoon show) and Nightmare Fuel: Blood Noir (stage show), have their own fixed showtimes each night. The Shows page in the hamburger menu lists them out, with the next upcoming showtime highlighted in red and already-passed ones grayed out.

Change parks

This tracker covers both Universal Studios Florida (Orlando) and Universal Studios Hollywood. You picked one when you first opened the tracker, and it's remembered on your device. Switch anytime from the Change Park option in the hamburger menu, or with the button below.
